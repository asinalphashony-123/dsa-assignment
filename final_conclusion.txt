The analysis shows that both approaches require O(n) time for evaluation. Stack-based postfix evaluation is the most suitable approach when only the final result is required because it is simple and direct. Expression tree evaluation is more suitable when the structure of the expression must be preserved, displayed, or traversed.

Therefore, the suitable approach depends on the requirement. For direct calculation, postfix stack evaluation is preferred; for structural analysis and multiple traversals, the expression tree is preferred.
